From SA-MP to FiveM: how GTA roleplay began

Roleplay · October 28, 2026 · 2 min read

Today GTA RP is a phenomenon with hundreds of thousands of viewers on Twitch. But it started much earlier, without any official support.

The beginning: mods that added multiplayer

Older GTA games had no online mode, so the community built one. Multi Theft Auto started around 2003, and in 2006 came San Andreas Multiplayer (SA-MP). SA-MP hosted the first big RP servers with rules, factions and commands like /me and /do that are still used today.

GTA V and a new generation

After GTA V launched in 2013, RP moved to Los Santos. Community platforms such as FiveM, RAGE:MP and alt:V let people run their own servers with custom rules, economies, jobs and cars. Players were no longer just "gangsters in the city" but also police officers, mechanics, lawyers and taxi drivers.

Twitch and NoPixel

The NoPixel server, founded in 2017, became a Twitch hit around 2019. Well-known streamers played their characters every day and viewers followed their stories like a TV series. It pushed GTA V back among the most-watched games on the platform, years after its release.

2023: Rockstar joins in

For a long time, RP sat in a grey area. In August 2023, Rockstar announced that the Cfx.re team behind FiveM and RedM had joined the company. It was the first clear sign that community roleplay is an important part of the game's life.

What about GTA VI?

We don't yet know whether or when GTA VI will support community RP servers. Rockstar hasn't announced anything, and the game's release doesn't mean servers will be available right away. What's certain is that the community will be ready. If you want to be among the first, start with the basics of roleplay.
